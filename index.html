❓ How to Play

You are a baby dinosaur. There is only one thing to work out, over and over: is that smaller than me?

  • Smaller than you — eat it! It fills your belly bar.
  • The same size as you — totally safe. You just bump noses.
  • Bigger than you — keep away. It costs a heart if it catches you.
  • Spiky ones — never food, whatever size you are. Leave them alone.

Fill the belly bar and you grow a size — and everything you used to run from turns into dinner. Berries and ferns are always safe to eat, so there is always something.

When something is close, the game tells you: a red ring means it can eat you, a yellow glow means you can eat it. Nothing in the valley can out-run you, and big hunters get bored after a few seconds.

Stars come from your hearts — finish without being caught for all three. Take as long as you like.
